NIGERIA JOLLOF RICE

A rich and flavorful one-pot Nigeria rice dish cooked in a tasty tomato and pepper sauce.

  • Servings: 6
  • Prep Time: 20 mins
  • Cook Time: 40 mins
  • Difficulty: Easy

Ingredients

  • 3 cups long grain parboiled rice
  • 6 medium tomatoes
  • 2 red bell peppers (tatashe)
  • 1 scotch bonnet pepper (ata rodo)
  • 1 large onion
  • 3 cloves garlic
  • 1/2 cup vegetable oil
  • 2 tbsp tomato paste
  • 2 tsp curry powder
  • 2 tsp thyme
  • 2 seasoning cubes
  • Salt to taste
  • 4 cups chicken or vegetable stock
  • Bay leaf (optional)

Instructions

  1. Blend tomatoes, red bell peppers, onion, garlic and scotch bonnet pepper until smooth.
  2. In a large pot, heat oil and fry the blended mixture on medium heat until the water dries up and the sauce is thick.
  3. Stir in tomato paste, curry powder, thyme, seasoning cubes, salt and bay leaf. Mix well.
  4. Pour in the stock and bring to a boil.
  5. Add the rice, stir, then reduce heat to low. Cover tightly and cook for about 25–30 minutes or until the rice is tender and the liquid is absorbed.
  6. Stir, fluff with a fork and serve hot.

Chef's Tip

For smoky flavor, cook with smoked turkey or kilishi. Avoid stirring too much to keep the rice separate and fluffy.
